Getting Started with the Tool:

  1. Upload Dataset: Go to the 1. Upload & Setup tab. Drag or click to upload your spreadsheet. If you are just testing, hit Load Simulated MEAL Data to test immediately.
  2. Review Profile: Review the KPI card summaries containing total column indicators, null count balances, and duplicate counts.
  3. Cleaning & Transform: Open the 2. Clean & Transform tab. Drop exact duplicates, select columns to fill empty cell rows, or expand the Power Query Transforms block to build conditional columns, execute math operations, run casing transformations, or split/merge columns!
  4. Process Pivots & Summaries: Move to 3. Process & Pivot to summarize aggregates like Mean, Sum, and Record Counts with live plotting.
  5. Draft Reports & Download: Inspect natural descriptions in 4. Reports & GIS Map, then download the structured multi-sheet Excel package on the final tab!
